Routers and Loops

Branch on a condition, catch everything else, and repeat steps over a list.

Route the run First match
Enterprise seats > 200 Alert the account owner
Business seats > 50 Queue a check-in
Trialing trial ends in 3 Book an onboarding call
Churn risk usage down 40% Open a save task
Self-serve plan is Free Send the upgrade nudge
Past due invoice unpaid Retry the charge
Otherwise nothing matched Log it and carry on
Loop on items Repeats every step above
