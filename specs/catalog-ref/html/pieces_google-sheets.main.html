About the Google Sheets integration

Add the Google Sheets piece to a flow, connect your account once, and the rest of the builder can read from it and write to it. Because the piece is open source you can run it on our cloud or self-host it, and combine Google Sheets with 760+ other apps in the same flow.

    Triggers when a new row is added or modified in a spreadsheet.

  • New Row Added
